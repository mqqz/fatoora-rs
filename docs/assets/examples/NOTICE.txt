These examples are for local development and demonstration. The certificate and
private key are public test credentials, not credentials for a user's business.

- signed.xml: fatoora-core/tests/fixtures/sdk-parity/cases/simplified-invoice/sdk-signed.xml
- sample-invoice.xml: fatoora-core/tests/fixtures/invoices/sample-simplified-invoice.xml
- certificate.der and private-key.der: fatoora-core/tests/fixtures/sdk-parity/credentials/
- certificate.pem and private-key.pem: PEM encodings of those same DER files
- previous-hash.txt and validation-options.json: predecessor value used by invoice.xml
